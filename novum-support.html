Novum Support

Email us any time: supportturtles@duck.com. We usually reply within two working days.

Does my scale work with Novum?

You'll know in the first minute of setup, free: step on your scale when Novum asks, and it will find it and log your first weigh-in. Novum is designed to work with many popular Bluetooth bathroom scales, including many models from Renpho, Etekcity and Eufy, but not every model. If yours isn't found, email supportturtles@duck.com with your scale's make and model. Novum is not affiliated with or endorsed by these brands; their names are trademarks of their owners.

Nothing happens when I step on

Open Novum first, then step on. Novum shows what it's doing under the circle and retries by itself; if it asks for help, tap the scale with your foot to wake it, close the scale's own app, and turn Bluetooth off and on.

Restore a purchase or move to a new phone

Use "Restore purchase" on the unlock screen or in Settings, signed in to the same Google or Apple account. To bring your weigh-ins along, turn on a backup in Settings → Your data before you switch.

Cancel a subscription

Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Novum. iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Novum. You keep access until the period you paid for ends.

Get a copy of my data

Settings → Your data → Export my weigh-ins gives you a spreadsheet. It's always free, even without a subscription.
