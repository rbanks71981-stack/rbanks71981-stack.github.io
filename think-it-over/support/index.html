Think It Over: Support

Need a hand? Write to supportturtles@duck.com. A real person reads every message, usually within a couple of days.

It helps to tell us which phone you use (iPhone or Android), the app version (shown in your App Store or Google Play listing), and what happened.

Reading and talking

Every question is free to read, with nothing to sign up for. To talk one through, tap Think it over under the question.

The first time you do, a small sheet asks you to confirm that you are 18 or older and that what you write is sent to an AI service provider in the United States so it can reply. It also offers Share anonymous usage, which is off unless you tick it (or have already turned it on in the settings). Tap Start, or Not now to keep reading. Talking it through needs a subscription, so if you do not have one, the app then shows you the plans.

Limits

Each subscription can have up to 100 conversations a day, 200 replies a day and 1,500 replies a month. Days run from midnight to midnight in the time zone your phone was set to when you first used the app (after 90 days without use, the next use counts as the first), and months from the 1st in that same time zone. Every phone on the same subscription shares them. If you reach one, the app tells you when it lifts. The Terms of Use explain more.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple or Google, so payment, cancelling and refunds all happen in your store account.

Cancel or change a subscription

iPhone

  1. Open the Settings app.
  2. Tap your name, then Subscriptions.
  3. Tap Think It Over, then Cancel Subscription.

Android

  1. Open the Google Play Store app.
  2. Tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions.
  3. Tap Think It Over, then Cancel subscription.

If you are subscribed, you can also tap the gear at the bottom of the question or conversation screen, then Cancel or change subscription, to go straight to your store's page.

Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

After you cancel you keep access until the end of the period you have paid for. If you are on a free trial, cancel before it ends to avoid being charged (on an iPhone, at least 24 hours before it ends).

Restore a subscription

On a new phone, or after reinstalling the app, sign in to the same Apple or Google account you subscribed with, open the plans screen (tap Think it over under any question) and tap Restore purchase.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by the store, not by us:

  • App Store: request one at https://reportaproblem.apple.com
  • Google Play: see https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637

Report a reply

If a reply from the thinking partner is wrong, upsetting or inappropriate, please report it from the conversation screen. Pick a reason and, if you like, add a note. We read every report and use them to make the partner better. Reports are kept for 180 days. If something is serious or you want an answer, email us as well.

The partner is an AI. It can be wrong, and it is not a source of medical, legal, financial or other professional advice.

If you are going through something hard

Think It Over is for thinking about questions in the abstract. It is the wrong tool if you are struggling with something real. In Canada and the US you can call or text 988 at any hour. Elsewhere, please contact your local emergency number or crisis line.

Your data

There are no accounts, and your past conversations are saved only on your phone. Our Privacy Policy explains exactly what reaches our server and how long it is kept: https://supportturtles.com/think-it-over/privacy

Usage sharing is off unless you turn it on. You can switch it on or off at any time with the gear at the bottom of the question and conversation screens.

Deleting your data. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your phone. What our server holds is tied only to a random ID the app made up, not to your name, and is deleted on the schedule in the Privacy Policy. If you want it deleted sooner, or want a copy of it, email supportturtles@duck.com with the subject "Delete my data" or "Copy of my data". Include your install ID, which is shown at the bottom of the app's settings (the gear). We reply within 30 days.

The person accountable for protecting personal information at Support Turtles is its owner, the developer, at supportturtles@duck.com. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (https://www.priv.gc.ca) or the privacy authority where you live.
