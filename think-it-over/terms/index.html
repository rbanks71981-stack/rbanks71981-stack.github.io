1. What the app is

Think It Over offers thought experiments and puzzles about how we think. Reading the questions is free and needs nothing from you. Talking a question through with the AI thinking partner needs a subscription.

2. The thinking partner is an AI

The partner's replies are written by an artificial intelligence model run by our AI service provider, a company in the United States, which our server sends your conversation to. It is not a person.

  • AI replies can be wrong. They can state things that are not true, misremember who said what, or reason badly. Check anything that matters to you.
  • It is not professional advice. Nothing the partner says is medical, psychological, legal, financial or any other professional advice. The questions are for thinking, not for deciding real situations in your life.
  • It is not a crisis service. If you are going through something difficult or are in danger, contact local emergency services or a crisis line. In Canada and the US you can call or text 988.

3. Age

You must be 18 or older to talk a question through with the AI partner. The first time you tap "Think it over", the app asks you to confirm this, and to agree that what you write is sent to an AI service provider in the United States so it can reply. By confirming, you tell us that it is true. Reading the questions does not need this.

5. Fair use and limits

To keep the service running for everyone at a price we can sustain, each subscription has fair-use limits:

  • up to 100 conversations a day
  • up to 200 replies from the partner a day
  • up to 1,500 replies a month
  • a single conversation runs for a set number of replies

Days run from midnight to midnight in the time zone your phone was set to when you first used the app (after 90 days without use, the next use counts as the first), and months from the 1st in that same time zone. The limits apply to the subscription, so every phone that uses it shares them. When you reach one of the daily or monthly reply or conversation limits, the app tells you when it lifts. We can change these limits only as section 13 describes. The service may sometimes be unavailable, for example for maintenance or if our AI provider has an outage.
