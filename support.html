How do I play?

Pick a character and a song, tap Play, then put your phone in your pocket. Roll your hips to keep the invisible hoop going and stay with the rhythm. Your phone's motion sensors feel the movement, so the screen can stay in your pocket the whole round.

What is Camera mode?

Camera mode films you hooping and draws the hoop on the video, with music. Prop the phone up where it can see all of you and use the front camera, or hand it to a friend to film you with the rear camera. Keep your hands on your hips, at chest height or over your head so they stay clear of the hoop. The video stays on your phone unless you save or share it. Camera mode is on Android now and coming to iPhone.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the Upgrades tab and tap Restore purchases (it is also in Settings). Use the same account you bought with: your Google account on Android, or your Apple ID on iPhone. Purchases made on Android cannot be moved to iPhone, or the other way round, because Google and Apple handle them separately.

Can I play with friends?

Yes, with "Play together" on the Play tab. It connects phones that are close to each other, with no internet needed. It works iPhone to iPhone or Android to Android, but not between an iPhone and an Android phone.
