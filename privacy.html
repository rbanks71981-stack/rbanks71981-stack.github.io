Privacy policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026

uHoop Air ("the app", "we") is a game that turns your phone into an invisible hoop. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. We do not run any servers, and we do not collect, sell or rent your personal information.

No account, no sign-up

You can play without creating an account. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, age or location.

Information that stays on your phone

  • Motion sensors. The app reads your phone's motion sensors while you play, to follow the movement of your hips. These readings are processed on your phone in real time and are not stored or sent anywhere.
  • Your scores and settings: your rounds, best times, chosen character, sound settings and similar preferences. They are stored on your phone. If you use Android's backup, Android may include them in your own Google backup. The optional hooper name you choose for share cards is never included in backups.
  • Purchases. Paid characters, songs and "No ads" are bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your payment details. The app only learns which items you own.

Playing with friends nearby

When you join or host a room with "Play together", the app uses Google Nearby Connections to connect directly to phones close to you (Bluetooth and Wi-Fi). Only what the game needs is shared with the other players in the room: your hooper name (if you set one), your chosen character, your game progress and scores for that room, and a random game ID that lets you rejoin a room. This goes directly between the phones; it does not pass through our servers. Your phone may ask for "Nearby devices" or location permission so it can find other phones; the app does not use your location for anything else.

Invites

If you install the app from a friend's invite link, the app reads the invite's room code from Google Play (the Play install referrer) so it can open that room.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show ads, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address and approximate location, and how you interact with ads, according to Google's privacy policy and its page on how Google uses information from apps that use its services. In regions where the law requires consent (such as the European Economic Area and the UK), the app asks for your choice before ads use your information, and you can change it at any time in Settings › Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Buying "No ads" removes the ads shown between rounds; optional ads you choose to watch for a reward stay available.

Sharing

The app never shares anything on its own. When you tap Share, it creates an image of your score and hands it to the app you choose (for example, a messaging app). What happens next is up to you and that app.

Children

uHoop Air is intended for players 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are limited to content suitable for teens.

Your choices

  • Change your ad consent in Settings › Privacy options (where shown).
  • Remove the app's data from your phone at any time by clearing its storage or uninstalling it.
  • Because we hold no personal information about you on any server, there is nothing for us to export or delete; ad information is managed by Google (see the links above).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: use the developer contact details shown on the uHoop Air listing on Google Play.
