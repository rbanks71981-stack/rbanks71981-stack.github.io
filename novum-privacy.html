Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026. Novum is made by Support Turtles ("we"). Questions: supportturtles@duck.com

The short version

Your weigh-ins, body measurements and answers stay on your phone. We don't have servers that receive them, we don't have accounts, and we don't sell or share your data. We see something only if you choose to email us a problem report. There are no ads and no tracking.

What Novum stores, and where

Everything below is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone:

  • Weigh-ins from your Bluetooth scale (weight, the time, and, if your scale sends them, foot-pad measurements and the scale's own readings).
  • What you enter: units, sex, height, birth date, goals, reminder time, answers to the lifestyle questions, and any DEXA scan results you add.
  • Your settings, and whether you have unlocked Novum.

Novum's own backups are off by default. If you turn on phone backup ("Back up with my phone's Google backup" on Android) or folder backup ("Save backups to a folder"), a copy of your Novum data goes to your own Google account or to the folder you chose (for example in Google Drive, iCloud Drive or Dropbox), under that company's terms. On iPhone, if you back up your phone to iCloud or a computer, Apple includes Novum's data in that backup, as it does for every app; you turn that on or off in your iPhone's settings, and it is kept in your own Apple account or computer under Apple's terms. We never receive any of these copies.

What Novum reads from your phone

  • Bluetooth, to hear and connect to your smart scale. On older Android versions, Android requires the location permission for Bluetooth scanning; Novum does not use or store your location.
  • Notifications (Android), only for the daily reminder, if you choose one.

Payments

Purchases and subscriptions are handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. Novum's only network connection is to the store, through Google Play Billing or Apple's StoreKit, to show prices, make a purchase and check whether you have one. We never see your card or payment details. The store tells Novum only whether you have an active purchase. On Android, Google's billing software may send Google its own diagnostic information about the purchase process, under Google's privacy policy; it receives no health data from Novum.

Sharing and exports

Novum only sends your data somewhere when you ask it to: "Export my weigh-ins" and "Back up everything" create a file and hand it to your phone's share sheet, and you choose where it goes.

Problem reports

If something goes wrong, Novum can offer to send us a problem report. It shows you every word of the report first, then opens your own email app addressed to us; nothing is sent unless you press Send. A report contains no weigh-in history, body fat, height, birth date, sex, goals, DEXA results or names, and Bluetooth addresses are shortened. A report about a wrong weight includes that one weigh-in, and a report about your scale includes a few raw readings broadcast by your scale (and by nearby Bluetooth devices), which can contain weigh-ins. On iPhone, Apple's on-device crash detection (MetricKit) lets Novum notice a crash and offer a report the next time you open it. We use reports only to fix the problem, and you can ask us to delete your email at any time.

Deleting your data

Settings → Your data → Delete all my data erases it from the app. Uninstalling Novum also deletes it from your phone (except any copy in your own phone backup or backup folder, as above, which you can delete there). We hold none of your data, apart from any problem report you emailed us; email us and we will delete it.

Children

Novum is designed for adults and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly receive any information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above. If a change ever meant your data leaving your phone, we would ask you first in the app.
