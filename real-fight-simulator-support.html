How do I play?

Hold your phone up with both hands, like a boxer's guard, and point the camera at your opponent's face. Boxing gloves appear over their arms. Flick up to punch, flick diagonally across for a hook, and hold and drag a glove to block. A punch that gets past their gloves scores a point. First to 10 wins.

How do two players connect?

You both need the game. Stand about 2 metres apart. One of you taps HOST GAME, the other taps JOIN GAME and picks the host's name.

  • Two Android phones connect directly. Android asks for Nearby devices (and on older versions, Location) permission. The game never uses your location.
  • Two iPhones connect directly. Allow Local Network when asked.
  • iPhone vs Android: both phones must be on the same Wi-Fi, or one phone turns on its hotspot and the other joins it. Guest and public Wi-Fi often block phones from seeing each other.

Can't find the other phone?

Check that both phones have the latest version, that the permissions above are allowed (Settings → Apps → Real Fight Simulator), and for iPhone vs Android, that both are on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot.

I bought something but it isn't unlocked

Open POWER-UPS and tap RESTORE PURCHASES. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID or Google account, so use the same one you bought with.

Refunds

Payments are handled by Apple and Google. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com (iPhone) or through your Google Play order history (Android).

Play safe

Give yourselves room, play somewhere with nothing to trip over, and no real punches.
