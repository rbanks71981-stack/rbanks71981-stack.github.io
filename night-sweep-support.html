Common questions

Will the scan ever find a monster or a ghost?
No. Never. There is no setting, no random chance and no hidden mode. Every sweep ends in an all-clear. That is the whole point of the app.

The sweep is slow, or the bar is not moving much.
Hold the phone up and turn slowly all the way around, tipping it up high and down low. The sweep follows the phone's motion. If the phone is lying still it still finishes on its own, within about thirty seconds, so it can never get stuck.

How do I change the places in our room?
Tap “Change the places” on the sweep screen (it shows for the first few seconds), or on the zapper screen after the shield is up.

How do I turn the voice off?
On the screen where you pick the places, tap the Night Sweep name at the top to open the grown-ups' settings.

Why does the app open straight on the zapper?
After the night shield goes up, the app opens on the zapper for twelve hours, so a child who wakes in the night does not have to sweep the room again in the dark. The next evening it starts fresh.

Does the app need the internet or any permissions?
No. It works in airplane mode, collects nothing and has no ads. See the privacy policy.
