RFReal Fight Simulator

Privacy policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

The short version

This policy covers Real Fight Simulator on Android (Google Play) and on iPhone (the App Store). Both versions work the same way, and everything below applies to both unless a section says otherwise.

We do not run any servers. We do not collect, sell or rent your personal information. The camera picture is processed on your phone and is never uploaded. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some information to do that (see "Ads" below).

Who we are

Real Fight Simulator is published by Support Turtles Unlimited. You can reach us at supportturtles@duck.com.

No account, no sign-up

You can play without creating an account. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, age or location.

The camera

The app uses your phone's back camera to show your opponent and draw boxing gloves over their arms. To place the gloves, it finds your opponent's face in each camera frame. This face detection runs entirely on your phone, in real time. It only finds where a face is in the picture. It does not recognise who anyone is. The camera picture and the detection results are not recorded, stored or sent anywhere, except for the fight photos described below.

Fight photos (2-player mode)

In a 2-player match, each phone's camera points at the other player, so the best pictures of you are on your opponent's phone. During the match, each phone keeps up to 3 pictures of its opponent in memory. Each picture shows the camera image and the game's gloves.

  • When the match ends, the loser's phone sends its 3 pictures, which show the winner, straight to the winner's phone over the local connection between the two phones, then forgets them. The pictures never go through the internet or to us.
  • The winner can pick one to put on a share card, or choose "No thanks". The pictures are kept only in memory and are not saved to either phone.
  • A share card is saved to your phone only if you tap Share: on Android to Pictures/Real Fight Simulator, on iPhone only where you choose in the share sheet. It leaves your phone only if you pick an app to send it to.

Only play 2-player with people who are happy to be photographed this way.

Playing with a friend nearby

2-player mode connects two phones directly, over Bluetooth or Wi-Fi with Google Nearby Connections on Android, or over the same Wi-Fi network or hotspot. Game moves, the name of your phone (so your friend can pick it), the power-ups you own and any gifts you send go only to the other phone. Nothing goes through our servers, because we do not have any.

Information that stays on your phone

Your progress and settings stay on your phone: levels reached, your best Aura Points, power-ups you own, pending gifts and similar preferences. They may be included in your own Google, iCloud or device backup, like other apps' data. That backup is yours and is never sent to us.

On Android, the match announcer uses your phone's own text-to-speech engine.

Purchases

Power-ups and ad-free upgrades are bought through Google Play on Android and through the App Store on iPhone. Google or Apple handles the payment, and we never see your payment details. The app only learns which items you own, so it can unlock them.

Ads

Unless you buy the ad-free upgrade, the app shows banner and full-screen ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information from your device, such as your advertising ID (on Android), IP address, device type and how you interact with ads. Google's use of this information is covered by How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

  • Europe, UK and Switzerland: before any ads are requested, the app shows Google's consent form so you can choose how your data is used for ads. You can change your choice later with the AD PRIVACY button on the home screen.
  • iPhone: the app does not ask for permission to track you and does not use Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings, under Privacy → Ads.

Ad-free owners: the app does not start the ads SDK at all.

Permissions the app asks for

Each permission is requested only when needed. If you refuse one, only the feature that needs it stops working.

  • Camera: to play. See "The camera" above.
  • Nearby devices / Bluetooth / Wi-Fi (Android) or Local Network (iPhone): only for 2-player mode, to find your friend's phone. On older Android versions this shows up as a location permission. The app does not use your location for anything.
  • Notifications (Android): to show the fight card from your last match if you leave the app right after it.

Children

Real Fight Simulator is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, delete and port your personal information. We hold no personal information about you, so we have nothing to produce or erase. Uninstalling the app (or, on Android, clearing its data in Settings) removes everything it stored on your phone. For information Google holds for ads, use Google's own controls linked above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update it here and change the effective date above.
