NSNight Sweep

Privacy policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026

The short version

Night Sweep does not collect any information about you or your child. It has no internet permission, sends nothing anywhere, has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. It works in airplane mode, permanently. There is no data for us to lose, sell, share or hand over, because we never receive any.

Who we are

Night Sweep is published by Support Turtles Unlimited. You can reach us at supportturtles@duck.com.

What we collect

Nothing.

We do not collect, receive, transmit or store any personal information, and that includes:

  • No name, email address, phone number, date of birth or age
  • No account or login of any kind — the app has no sign-up
  • No advertising ID, device ID, Android ID or any other identifier
  • No location data, coarse or precise
  • No contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera access
  • No analytics, usage statistics, crash reports or telemetry
  • No cookies or similar technologies

Permissions the app requests

None. Night Sweep requests no permissions at all. It does not request internet access, so it is technically incapable of sending information off your device.

(Your device may show one internal permission belonging to the app itself, created automatically by Google's standard AndroidX libraries so the app's own components can talk to each other. It grants no access to anything and is not visible or usable outside the app.)

What is stored on your device

Four things, all kept in the app's private storage on your own device:

  1. Which places you ticked on the setup screen — for example "under the bed" or "the closet" — so you do not have to choose them again every night.
  2. A marker recording that setup has been completed, so the app can go straight to the sweep.
  3. The time the night shield was last put up, so that if the app is opened again during the night it returns to where you left off instead of starting over. This is an elapsed time only — it is not a location, not an account, and not linked to anything.
  4. Whether the spoken voice is switched on or off, so your choice is remembered.

That is the entire contents. None of it identifies anyone. It never leaves your device, we never see it, and it is permanently deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in your device's settings.

Motion sensors

To run a sweep, the app reads your device's rotation and tilt sensors so it can tell when you have turned all the way around the room. These readings are used in the moment, held only in memory, and discarded as soon as the sweep ends. They are never saved to storage and never transmitted. The app does not use the camera or the microphone.

The spoken voice

If narration is switched on, Night Sweep passes its own fixed phrases — lines like "Tip it down at the bed" — to the text-to-speech service built into your device. Those phrases are written by us and contain no information about you or your child.

We want to be straightforward about one thing: that text-to-speech service is a separate part of your device, usually supplied by your phone's manufacturer or by Google, and it operates under its own privacy policy rather than ours. Depending on your device and settings, it may use a network connection of its own to produce higher-quality voices. We do not control it and we receive nothing from it. The voice can be turned off inside Night Sweep – open the setup screen and tap the app's name at the top. With it off the app is silent and the same words stay on screen. The voice also follows your device's normal volume, and your device's text-to-speech engine can be changed or switched off entirely in your device's accessibility settings.

Children's privacy

Night Sweep is designed for young children and is intended to be used with a parent or carer.

Because we collect no personal information at all, there is nothing for a child to disclose to us and nothing for a parent to consent to, review or request the deletion of. We do not knowingly or unknowingly collect personal information from children, as we do not collect personal information from anyone.

The app contains no advertising of any kind, no personalised advertising, no in-app purchases, no in-app messaging, no social features, no user-generated content, no external links reachable by a child, and no way to contact anyone through it. This is intended to satisfy the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU and UK GDPR including their provisions on children, the UK Age Appropriate Design Code, and Google Play's Families policy.

Third parties

Night Sweep contains no third-party advertising networks, analytics providers, crash-reporting services, social media integrations or marketing tools. The app is built using Google's standard Android and Jetpack libraries, which run on your device and send nothing to anyone.

We do not share, sell, rent or disclose information to anyone, because we hold none.

Payments

Night Sweep is bought once, up front. There are no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, no upgrades and nothing inside the app that asks for money. A child using it cannot spend anything, because there is nothing in it to buy.

The purchase itself is handled entirely by the app store, not by us. We receive sales reports that contain no information identifying you, and the store's handling of your payment is covered by its own privacy policy rather than this one.

How long we keep information

We keep nothing, so there is no retention period. The small amount of information stored on your own device (above) stays there until you delete the app or clear its data.

Your rights

Laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, delete and port your personal information, and to object to its processing. We hold no personal information about you, so there is nothing for us to produce, correct or erase. The settings you have saved on your own device can be removed at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android's settings.

If you would like written confirmation that we hold nothing about you, write to supportturtles@duck.com and we will say so.

Data transfers

None. No information leaves your device, so nothing is transferred to another country.

Security

The strongest security measure available is not to collect anything, which is the approach we have taken. The settings stored on your device are held in the app's private storage, which Android protects from other apps.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes we will update it here and change the effective date above. If a future version of Night Sweep ever collects anything at all, we will update this policy before that version is released and describe plainly what it collects and why.
